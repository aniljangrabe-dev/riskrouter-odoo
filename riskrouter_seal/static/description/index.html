Seal every posted invoice, and keep a receipt anyone can check

Each posted customer invoice or credit note, and if you choose each vendor bill, is sealed in RiskRouter's append-only evidence log. A receipt is attached to the invoice, and anyone can check it offline with the published, open-source verifiers, without Odoo and without RiskRouter.

What leaves Odoo

The SHA-256 of a small salted record, and its kind. The record names the invoice by its number, its date and the SHA-256 of its PDF, and the customer by an id such as odoo-partner-42. No amount, no name, no email and no file is sent.

What a receipt shows, and what it does not

It shows that this record, naming this exact file, existed unchanged by the time the log sealed it. It does not show that the invoice is correct, that it was delivered, or that it meets any rule.

Set up

Accounting, Settings, Evidence seal (RiskRouter): paste an API key (a sandbox key needs no account, at riskrouter.eu/integrate) and choose what to seal. Entries in the log are permanent. If the log cannot be reached, the record is kept and sent on the next run; posting an invoice never waits for it.
